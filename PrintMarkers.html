IMR Lab 1 - AR Markers

Each marker must measure exactly 10 cm wide. The black bar under each image is exactly 100 mm — measure it with a ruler after printing!
⚠ Print at 100% scale / "Actual size" — do NOT use "Fit to page". If the bar is not exactly 10 cm, characters will misbehave (too far apart / wrong distances).
How to play: print → cut the two cards → point the phone at both cards (flat, 30-50 cm away) → characters appear on the markers → slide the cards closer than 25 cm apart → ATTACK mode (whoosh + sparks + HUD distance). Use the Model button (bottom-left) to switch Cacti → Unity-chan → Mixed.
